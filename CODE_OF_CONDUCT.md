# Community conduct

Communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas and code. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, and publication of private information are not accepted.
Respect speakers and people who stutter; avoid stigmatizing descriptions or
clinical claims unsupported by the research.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict further
participation. Report ordinary moderation issues to repository maintainers through
their publicly listed GitHub contact channels. Do not disclose private information
in a public issue. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
